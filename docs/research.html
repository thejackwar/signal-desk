We tested 87 strategies.

Popular trading ideas, tested the honest way against simply holding the same stocks. Here's every result, and why most of them didn't work.

How we tested

Every strategy had to beat the simplest thing you could do: buy all the stocks it picks from and hold them.

1Screen: 2021 to now

All 87 run on the 100 biggest US stocks of each moment (not today's winners), paying 0.10% every time they buy or sell.

2Re-test: 2018–2020

The ones that beat holding were run again on years that played no part in picking them. Luck rarely repeats.

3Count the luck

Try 87 ideas and a few will look great by pure chance, like coin-flippers on a lucky streak. So the bar goes up with every try.
