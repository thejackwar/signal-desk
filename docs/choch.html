The rules

Exactly what the engine does on every finished 4-hour candle.

1 · Structure

Find the swings

A swing high is a 4H candle higher than the 3 before and after it (pivot length). Lower highs and lower lows = downtrend.

2 · Change of character

Close above the last lower high

A body close, not a wick. A break with the trend (BOS) is ignored; only the first break against it counts.

3 · Entry

Buy the retest

Limit order in the middle of the fair value gap the move left (or the last red candle at the low). Valid for 10 candles.

4 · Exit

Stop under the low, target 2R

Stop just below the low before the break. Target twice the risk. Out after 60 candles at the latest.
