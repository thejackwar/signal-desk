The rules

Exactly what the backtest does on every New York session.

09:30

Gaps down

Opens at least 3% under yesterday's close. At least $10M traded a day on average.

09:30–09:35

Opening range

The high and low of the first 5-minute candle. No trade before it closes.

Until 09:45

Short the break

Sell when price trades just under the range low. If it trades above the range high first, the gap is filling: no trade.

Out

Stop, target, clock

Stop just above the range high, target 2× the risk, anything left closed at 10:30.

Account: up to 5 trades a day (the biggest gaps first), 20% of the account each, no leverage. With a 5× CFD every return and every drop is about 5× bigger. 0.03% cost each way. Stop checked before target when a candle hits both.
