Does price action actually work?

Levels, breakouts, flip zones, trend lines, break of structure, change of character, sweeps, supply and demand zones and fair value gaps, each turned into exact rules a computer can test. Rules are found on 2022 to July 2024. The last two years stay sealed until everything is chosen, then open once.

How to read a verdict: survived means the effect points the claimed way with an interval that excludes zero on QQQ, SPY agrees and both eras agree (or, in the final test, it held on both markets). Split means only part of that holds. With many tests, a few will pass by luck; that is why the last two years stay sealed.

Data: Alpaca 1-minute bars of QQQ and SPY (all US exchanges, split- and dividend-adjusted), regular hours 09:30 to 16:00 New York. QQQ stands in for Nasdaq 100 futures and SPY for S&P 500 futures. Every trade is 100 shares, pays $1 commission per round trip and 1 cent of slippage on market orders and stops; limits fill only when price trades a cent through them, and a candle that hits both the stop and the target counts as a loss. News days are CPI, jobs reports and FOMC statements from the BLS and Federal Reserve calendars. Code and every saved result: research/pa_lab. Backtests are not a promise of future results.
