Getting started

Technical analysis reads price and volume only. The tools come in a few families; most traders use one or two from each, not all of them.

Trend: is it going up?

Moving averages, Supertrend, Parabolic SAR, Ichimoku and ADX. Price above a rising 200-day average is the classic "uptrend".

Momentum: how hard?

RSI, MACD, Stochastic and CCI. They show when a move is speeding up or tiring. "Overbought" isn't a sell signal by itself in a strong trend.

Volatility: how wild?

Bollinger and Keltner bands, Donchian channels and ATR. Narrow bands often come before a big move; ATR helps size a stop loss.

Levels: where might it stop?

Trendlines, support and resistance, Fibonacci, pivot points and the previous high/low. Prices often react near them; a clean break is a signal in itself.

Volume: is anyone behind it?

Volume, VWAP and OBV. A breakout on heavy volume is more convincing than one on light volume.
